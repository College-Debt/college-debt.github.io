We have a limited quantity of Microsoft 365 subscriptions available at no additional charge that you can add onto your account. This gives you access to:

* Full license to Microsoft Office productivity suite (Word, Excel, PowerPoint, etc.)
* Custom domain supported Microsoft Outlook mailbox

It's almost equivalent to a [Microsoft 365 Business Standard](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/business/microsoft-365-business-standard) subscription without the Copilot add-on or OneDrive storage. You may claim this complementary benefits if you really want any of the stuff above.
