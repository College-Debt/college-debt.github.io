Players can upload custom images straight through the site and turn them into in-game Minecraft paintings without having to mess with resource pack zips or GitHub issues anymore.

{{< hint "info" >}}
You'll need to sign in with your **College Debt Microsoft account** to publish or edit paintings. You can sign in using the key icon at the top right or directly from the [Custom Paintings](/paintings/) page.
{{< /hint >}}

## Uploading & publishing paintings

1. Head over to the [Custom Paintings](/paintings/) page and make sure you're signed in.
2. Drop your images into the upload box (up to **10 paintings** at a time; supports `PNG`, `JPG`, `WebP`, and `GIF`).
3. Tweak each painting to your liking before publishing:
   * **Crop & zoom:** Drag the preview around or use the sliders to frame your image cleanly inside the block dimensions.
   * **Size:** Use the **Size Grid** or **Width / Height** buttons to pick anywhere from `1×1` up to `4×4` blocks.
   * **Detail Scale & Scaling:** Use `1×` or `2×` to blend in with vanilla Minecraft textures, or raise it up to `8×` if you want crisp high-res images. Pick **Smooth** for photos/artwork or **Crisp** for pixel art.
   * **Frame Style:** Choose any Minecraft wood border or **Frameless**.
   * **Painting Title & Author:** Give your painting a title and author name (defaults to your display name).
4. Hit **Publish Paintings** when everything looks right.

## Managing paintings later

If you want to tweak something later, switch to the **Server Gallery** tab on the same [Custom Paintings](/paintings/) page. From there you can browse existing server paintings, click thumbnails to inspect them, rename titles/authors, or remove paintings you uploaded.

## Witness your artwork in-game

Once you publish, the server resource & data pack rebuilds automatically. After the server picks up the updated pack (restart or pack reload), grab an in-game Painting and place/cycle through it until yours shows up!

As an easier way, put the Painting item into a Stonecutter to directly select whatever painting you want.

## Bedrock Edition

Custom paintings are not supported on Bedrock clients due to technical limitation. Players on this version will instead see a 1x1 Kebab painting in place of the custom artwork.
